Inside AquaGuard AI

Two EfficientNetB0 models, trained with transfer learning in TensorFlow/Keras and converted to TensorFlow Lite, screen fish photos on the phone without needing internet.

Fish photo
Fish validation
Disease classification
Result and next steps
Model 1

Fish disease classifier

5,257images, 7 classes
Train 3,690Val 795Test 772
  • Public datasets (MatsyaDx-BD and Fish Disease Detection) combined, labels standardised
  • 224 × 224 RGB input, class weighting for imbalance
  • Augmentation: flip, rotation, zoom, contrast, brightness
  • Stage 1: frozen backbone, new head. Stage 2: last 80 layers fine-tuned at a low learning rate
  • Dropout, early stopping and learning-rate reduction
0test accuracy (756 of 772 images)
Bacterial Red DiseaseAeromoniasisBacterial Gill DiseaseSaprolegniasisParasitic DiseasesWhite Tail DiseaseHealthy Fish
Model 2

Fish or not fish

12,090images, balanced
Fish 6,045Not fish 6,045
  • Binary EfficientNetB0 that checks every photo before it reaches the disease model
  • Not-fish images come from TensorFlow Flowers (3,670) and CIFAR-10 (2,375)
  • Tested on 1,814 held-out images
≈99%test accuracy

This likely overstates real-world performance, because flowers and CIFAR-10 are easier than murky ponds, nets and hands. Harder negative examples are next.

Water monitoring9 parameters (pH, dissolved oxygen, temperature, ammonia, turbidity and more) with safe ranges from FAO, WorldFish, CIFA and NACA guidelines.
Pond healthRisk level, health score, daily feed estimates, weekly growth charts and a history timeline.
Bangla AI assistantGemini-powered answers by text or voice, with an offline rule-based fallback.
BackendFlask API on Render, plus a PHP service with the BDApps API for OTP and subscription.

Results come from public-dataset testing, not field trials. The app gives preliminary screening, not a veterinary diagnosis. Next: class-wise precision, recall and F1, TFLite speed benchmarks, and field testing with farmers.
